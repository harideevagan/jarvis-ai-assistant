About JARVIS AI

JARVIS is the AI assistant of Harideevagan. It is a chat website where visitors can ask questions about Harideevagan's work, services and projects in plain language.

What JARVIS can do

  • Answer questions about Harideevagan's AI, Odoo ERP and automation work.
  • Reply in the language you choose, including English, Tamil and Hindi.
  • Take voice input and read replies aloud, in browsers that support it.
  • Show formatted replies that you can copy or share.
  • Help you describe a project you need built so Harideevagan can follow up.
